01 What we ship today

One promise: upload a short video in any of 22 languages — leave with English subtitles burned into the file, every line human-reviewed by the user in a built-in editor. No accounts, no installs, one page.

UPLOAD ≤7 min · auto-compress >15 MB → WHISPER translate → .srt → EDIT player + cue editor → BURN ffmpeg, re-apply ∞
  • Honest by design: conditions shown as a conditions.srt file on the landing page; live queue position; files auto-delete in 24 h.
  • Resource-tight: one worker, one job at a time; closing the tab kills the user's running job — zero wasted CPU-hours.
  • Self-hosted stack: Node + Whisper + ffmpeg in one Docker container behind nginx. No per-request AI API fees.

02 Why short videos, why now

Short vertical video is the default publishing format, and most of it is watched on mute — platforms consistently report the majority of feed video plays without sound. Subtitles are not an accessibility extra; they are the difference between a view and a skip. Creators posting across borders need the same clip to work in English feeds.

Our wedge: the review step. Pure auto-caption tools ship Whisper's raw output; professional editors are heavyweight. We sit exactly between — machine speed, human final word, burned-in result.

The single-worker design caps burn rate by construction: costs cannot spike faster than one CPU can compute. A GPU node (~$150–300/mo) lifts throughput ~10–30× and cuts wait times from ×3–5 realtime to near-realtime — this is the "faster, no queues" upgrade the donation link already sells.

07 Risks — named, not hidden

  • Platform absorption: TikTok/YouTube auto-captions improve constantly. Our defense is the edit-and-burn workflow and multi-platform output, not raw transcription.
  • Abuse of free tier: IP limits are trivially rotated. Acceptable at MVP scale; move limits to nginx + persistent store when uploads/day pass ~200.
  • In-memory state: restarts drop queue & sessions by design. Fine for one box; revisit before any multi-node story.
  • Content liability: we process user videos for 24 h. Add a visible acceptable-use line + DMCA contact before marketing pushes.
